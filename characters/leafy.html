Leafy

Species: Leafeon

Occupation: Pizza shop owner

About

Leafy always seems to have a smile on their face. As the owner of Poke Pizza, Leafy does their best to satisfy the customers and bring in new ones. Leafy's super outgoing, and works hard to bring a positive environment wherever they go. After all, mon come for the pizza, but stay for the vibes.

Trivia

  • Leafeons are known to have very sharp tails. This could be used as a weapon if needed, but Leafy is more likely to use it to slice pizza.
  • Poke Pizza is a very small business, with only one location and very few employees, but is a place most the town knows and loves. With some nice background music and a bunch of seating, it's a common gathering place for mon of all types.
  • With such a large focus on outreach, and his bubbly personality, not much gets on Leafy's nerves. (although there might still be a few things)
