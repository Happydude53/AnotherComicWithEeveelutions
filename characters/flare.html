Flare

Species: Flareon

Occupation: Firefighter

About

Flare is extremely dedicated and determined. The type of mon to never back down, once Flare sets their mind to something, there's almost no stopping them.

Trivia

  • Flareons are known to enjoy roasting their food before they eat it. They also fluff out their fur to cool themselves down.
  • The firefighting squad is quite competitive, with multiple teams of firefighters that train their skills with and against each other. Due to the relatively small amount of fire incidents in the area, the majority of Flare's days are spent training.
  • Flare is the type of mon to ask for something super specific and customized in a store, and not bat an eye about it.
