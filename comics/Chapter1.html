Hello! Thanks for checking out my comic! I hope you enjoy these silly little guys that I’m drawing. I’m basically a complete beginner when it comes to art, but I’m sure I’ll improve over time. I was actually considering trying a different project involving art before this, but realized I absolutely could not draw well (and I would have needed to). So, here we are, learning, and what better way than to make a comic page (or multiple if people like it)? Anyways, Each of the eeveelutions will get some love, so don’t panic if you don’t see your favourite right away. Each will have their own stories, goals, and fears, often inspired by my life, my friends, or just whatever I think would be funny. Anyways, more yapping later.
